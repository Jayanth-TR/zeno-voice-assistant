## 3. Agentic AI & Individual AI Agents

### Zeno, Your Marketing Assistant
- **What it is:** Zeno is the marketing assistant marketers interact with via natural language. It understands business objectives and coordinates the appropriate specialized agents to execute the job.
- **Former Designation:** Unified Co-marketer. (The Netcore website still uses the "Co-Marketer" label in several product sections).
- **Workflow:** You ask a question or state a goal $\rightarrow$ Zeno understands the context $\rightarrow$ Zeno brings in the right specialized agent $\rightarrow$ You receive a grounded answer, segment, or report back.
- **Live Capabilities Today:**
  - Operates across campaigns, journeys, and segments within a unified conversation context.
  - Diagnoses root causes, uncovers behavioral audience cohorts, and surfaces journey bottlenecks.
  - Builds segments from a single sentence (Reachable Users card $\rightarrow$ View $\rightarrow$ Save to CDP).
  - Conducts Deep Research: prepares an analysis plan, followed by a 3 to 6 screen report with downloadable PDF, coverage metadata, and automated email/schedule delivery.
  - Appends Evidence Tags on all answers: *"Grounded in data"* or *"Projected"*.
  - Performs pre-flight health checks before any marketing campaign is dispatched.
  - Analyzes user-level send-time patterns and communication fatigue.
  - Generates brand-aware content and assists marketing teams in building custom agents.

### The Six Specialized Autonomous AI Agents
1. **Audience Agent:** Conversational customer-cohort discovery (translates natural language into predictive behavioral micro-segments).
2. **Content Agent:** Generates brand-aligned copy and creative assets adapted for Email (AMP), WhatsApp, RCS, Push, and SMS.
3. **Scheduler Agent:** Determines optimal send-time optimization (STO) and frequency pacing per individual customer to prevent communication fatigue.
4. **Insight Agent:** Continuously inspects journeys and campaigns, isolates drop-off causes, and provides actionable recommendations.
5. **Decisioning Agent:** Evaluates real-time signals (propensity, fatigue, stock, margins) to trigger the Next Best Action (NBA) or offer.
6. **Shopping Agent:** Powers conversational commerce on WhatsApp, RCS, and websites (product discovery, sizing, recommendations, and checkout).

## 4. Netcore Agent Hub & Custom Agents

### What is Agent Hub?
The central environment where AI agents operate. It features:
- **Agent Gallery:** Library of pre-built agents designed for standard marketing jobs.
- **Custom Agents:** Allows enterprise marketing teams to construct their own specialized agents for recurring analytical and operational workflows.

### What Custom Agents Can Do
- **Analysis-Only Scope:** Focused strictly on intelligence and diagnosis; does not initiate direct message sends, edit journeys, or modify existing customer segments without approval.
- **Data Access:** Reads campaigns, journeys, user segments, and historical performance metrics.
- **Specialized Modules:** Deep Research (required for generating structured PDF reports) and Brand Wiki (brand guidelines and tone rules).
- **Instruction Template:** Supports instructions up to 5,000 characters using the structured framework:
  1. *Role*
  2. *Reference Benchmarks*
  3. *Primary Rule*
  4. *What to Examine*
  5. *Output Format*
  6. *Rules & Boundaries*
- **Visibility:** Configurable as Public (team-wide) or Private.
- **Invocation:** Can be launched from the Agent Hub directory or initiated from *New Chat $\rightarrow$ + $\rightarrow$ Custom Agents*.
- **Automated Scheduling:** Recurring delivery of analysis via scheduled Deep Research reports.
- **Operational Boundaries:** No direct access to external raw CRM, finance, or web scrape data; no arbitrary file uploads; no real-time telemetry alerting; data refreshes on a scheduled sync cadence.
