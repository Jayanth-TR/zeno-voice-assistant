## What is agentic marketing?

The supplied material contrasts traditional marketing automation with an
agent-based approach.

Traditional marketing automation commonly relies on predefined IF-THEN
workflows. Marketers may need to manually build segments, prepare
content, configure timing, coordinate channels, and review campaign
performance.

The Netcore Agentic Marketing Platform is described in the source as
using goal-oriented AI agents. A marketer states a business objective
and sets relevant guardrails. Agents then support tasks such as
interpreting the objective, identifying audiences, generating
channel-specific content, planning delivery, and diagnosing campaign
performance.

## How the model works

The source describes a coordinated flow:

1.  **Set the strategic goal:** The marketer defines the business
    outcome.
2.  **Set guardrails:** Brand tone, audience constraints, compliance
    rules, and budget limits are specified.
3.  **Discover audiences:** The Audience Agent helps identify relevant
    customer cohorts.
4.  **Create content:** The Content Agent generates channel-specific
    creative variations.
5.  **Plan delivery:** Scheduler and Decisioning capabilities help
    determine timing, channel, and next action.
6.  **Review performance:** The Insight Agent diagnoses journey or
    campaign performance and surfaces possible improvements.

## Human control

The supplied material states that marketers retain control over campaign
objectives, audience guardrails, brand guidelines, and compliance rules.
Agents handle operational tasks within those boundaries.

Zeno should explain this as a human-guided operating model. Do not imply
that every action is fully autonomous or that approval steps are absent
unless the client confirms the exact product workflow.

## Example

A marketer might ask for help re-engaging customers who abandoned their
carts during the past week and whose average order value is above a
chosen threshold.

The source uses this type of natural-language instruction to illustrate
how the Audience Agent can translate a marketing goal into a customer
cohort.
